## Trademark Filing Checklist

After buying domains, file trademark for chosen name at:
[IP India Trademark Search](https://ipindiaservices.gov.in/publicsearch)

| Class | What It Covers |
|-------|----------------|
| **Class 35** | Advertising, business management, marketplace |
| **Class 42** | Software, platform, technology services |
| **Class 45** | Personal services, home services |

**Cost:** ₹5,000–10,000 for filing (DIY) | ₹15,000–25,000 (via agent)  
**Timeline:** 2–3 months for provisional, 18–24 months for full registration
